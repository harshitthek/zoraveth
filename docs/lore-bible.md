# Zoraveth Lore Bible

## Canon
Zoraveth is a fantasy world where a living ecosystem, forgotten civilizations, mysterious magic, and a vast frontier are connected by a deeper mystery. Its inhabitants have incomplete, conflicting accounts of history. Ordinary people know folklore, scholars debate evidence, and powerful groups possess more information without knowing the whole truth.

## Tone
Wonder, curiosity, danger and warmth. Ordinary life matters alongside epic mysteries. The world should reward observation, experimentation, research, preparation and brave exploration. Mystery should be intriguing rather than arbitrary.

## The four pillars
- **Living world:** Creatures occupy territories and respond to environmental conditions; settlements and factions have routines; time/weather/actions can alter availability. Simulation must be legible and affordable.
- **Forgotten civilizations:** Architecture, inscriptions, mechanisms and artifacts preserve evidence of earlier societies. Environmental storytelling is as important as text.
- **Mysterious magic:** Learnable spells coexist with advanced discoveries and personal soul powers. Nature, ancient civilizations and soul powers are connected somehow, but the final explanation is not canon.
- **Frontier:** Familiar settlements and mapped roads contrast with dangerous wilderness, underground civilizations, distant lands and hidden routes.

## Knowledge layers
1. Folklore: local stories, warnings, songs and rumours; some are accurate fragments.
2. Scholarship: competing theories based on incomplete records.
3. Powerful knowledge: orders, rulers, hidden societies and ancient beings may know more, conceal facts or misunderstand them.
4. Unknown: no one has the complete truth.

NPCs can be wrong. Players should be able to compare claims with evidence.

## Opening sequence
1. Cinematic battle involving a legendary being.
2. An inexplicable event occurs; the player cannot understand the whole event.
3. Player wakes in a small cabin near a forest.
4. A kind elderly stranger found them unconscious and helped them.
5. The stranger is warm and genuinely kind; subtle details hint at undisclosed knowledge.
6. Player explores, learns, gathers, fights a creature and makes an early discovery.
7. Much later, a clue from an astrologer or other source directs the player back to the cabin.
8. A hidden path exists beneath the house.
9. The exact revelation and stranger's role remain undecided.

## Protected mysteries
Do not define the final truth, the battle's nature, the stranger's identity, the path's destination, or whether the opening was a dream/memory/vision/literal event. Do not make every unrelated quest secretly about the central mystery. Establish fair clues before explanations.

## Naming
World: Zoraveth. Final game title and names of continents, factions, civilizations, languages, deities and legendary beings remain open. Use original names and check them before public release.
