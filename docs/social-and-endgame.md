# Social Systems and Endgame

Long-term design only; do not implement these before the local vertical slice is stable.

## Solo and group play
- Solo should support most exploration and progression.
- Group content should add meaningful cooperation for bosses, dungeons, guild challenges and world events.
- Support optional matchmaking alongside friend-organized parties.
- Group loot should be understandable and fair; personal progression can coexist with transparent shared rewards.

## Dungeons
- Combine combat, exploration, puzzles, story and secrets.
- Include both short and sprawling experiences.
- Major dungeons are handcrafted; repeatable expeditions can vary routes, hazards and enemies.
- Layer difficulty: baseline danger plus optional challenge modes/deeper layers.
- Preserve readable rules and fair access to important lore/progression.

## Raids and world battles
- Use instanced raids and open-world battles for different purposes.
- Support different group sizes with balanced prestige.
- World events should communicate danger and expectations; not every event needs to be mandatory.

## Guilds
- Guilds are communities with cooperative progression.
- Future features may include flexible governance, strongholds, events and regional influence.
- Build safeguards against permanent monopolies, abusive leadership and exclusion from core content.
- Guild systems need clear permissions and recovery paths before implementation.

## PvP, crime and bounty hunting
- Opt-in duels/arenas and selected contested regions/events.
- Protect ordinary areas.
- Make danger and death penalties clear before a player enters a risky context.
- Context-dependent crime and bounty systems may exist.
- Prevent harassment, exploitative spawn camping and ways to force unwilling players into PvP.

## Endgame
- Use layered long-term mastery, discoveries, social leadership, crafting, exploration, raids and evolving world content.
- Avoid endless numerical escalation that invalidates old accomplishments.
- Players can become known for combat, exploration, crafting, leadership, discoveries and world-first achievements.
- Recognition can include titles, NPC reactions, monuments and shared historical records. Exceptional titles may affect opportunities, but must not gate essential progression.

## Housing and community spaces
- Hybrid housing: instanced homes plus physical properties, guild halls and outposts where appropriate.
- Ownership can combine personal expression and prestige without permanent territorial monopolies.
- Lively social hubs should contrast with spacious wilderness and instanced discoveries.
